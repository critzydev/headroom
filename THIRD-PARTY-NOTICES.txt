DeepFilterNet
-------------
https://github.com/Rikorose/DeepFilterNet   Version 0.5.6, fetched by CMake
(cmake/HeadroomDfn.cmake). Copyright (c) 2021 Hendrik Schröter.
Licensed under either the MIT licence or the Apache License 2.0, at your option.

Headroom links its libDF library (together with the small C interface in
dfn-shim/) and ships its two noise-removal networks, DeepFilterNet3 and
DeepFilterNet3 low-latency, as files next to the program (the "Fast" and "Best"
noise removal). The networks are downloaded at build time, not stored in this
repository. The build copies the two licence files next to them
(LICENSE-DeepFilterNet-MIT.txt and LICENSE-DeepFilterNet-APACHE.txt in the
models folder), and the AppImage and tarball carry them along.

libDF runs the networks with tract (https://github.com/sonos/tract, MIT or
Apache-2.0). It and the other Rust crates the build pulls in (dfn-shim/Cargo.lock
lists them, with exact versions) are under their own licences.

The interface code in dfn-shim/ is Headroom's own, under the AGPL-3.0.

Speech clips used by the tests (tests/data/speech/)
---------------------------------------------------
Four short speech clips for the noise-removal tests (tests/unit/SpeechCutTests.cpp).
They are not part of the program and are not shipped with it.

They are taken from the CSTR VCTK Corpus (version 0.92):

    Yamagishi, Veaux and MacDonald, "CSTR VCTK Corpus: English Multi-speaker
    Corpus for CSTR Voice Cloning Toolkit (version 0.92)".
    University of Edinburgh, The Centre for Speech Technology Research (CSTR).
    https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2645

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the original FLAC files (wav48_silence_trimmed/<speaker>/
<speaker>_<utterance>_mic1.flac) were decoded to 16 bit PCM WAV. Nothing else
was changed: no resampling, no gain, no trimming. The clips are mono, 48 kHz,
9.5 s in all:

    file           speaker  voice  accent                     length
    p305_419.wav   p305     F      American (Philadelphia)    2.18 s
    p361_385.wav   p361     F      American (New Jersey)      2.24 s
    p334_190.wav   p334     M      American (Chicago)         2.48 s
    p304_267.wav   p304     M      Northern Irish (Belfast)   2.56 s

The tests mix these clips with noises that the tests generate themselves.


Protocols
---------
Headroom's USB control of the Elgato Wave:3 and (experimental) Wave XLR is
Headroom's own code, written from protocol descriptions that others published,
among them Kailas Mahavarkar's analysis of Elgato's Wave Link software and the
open-source projects rikkichy/openwave and emaspa/openxlr. Thanks to them.


System libraries
----------------
Headroom links PipeWire, FreeType, Fontconfig and the X11 libraries dynamically.
They come from the computer it runs on and are not bundled. The AppImage files
contain the AppImage runtime (https://github.com/AppImage/type2-runtime, MIT).
